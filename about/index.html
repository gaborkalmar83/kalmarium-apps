How we build

  • No account to start. Every app can be played or tried without signing up. Knowdrop only creates a simple online account if you pick a nickname for friends and leaderboards.
  • No forced subscription. Eight of the nine apps have no subscription. Knowdrop offers an optional Plus subscription, and it is never needed to play.
  • Small ads only. Where an app shows ads, they are small banners that stay out of the way, and a one-time purchase removes them. Bilingua and Crack A Fold have none.
  • Prices stated up front. What is free, what costs money and what an ad-free purchase does is written on this site and on each Google Play listing.
