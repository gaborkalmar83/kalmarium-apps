Privacy policy

For the website apps.kalmarium.com. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

This website is run by Kalmarium Services B.V., Almere, the Netherlands (“we”). We are the controller for the data described here.

In short

This website sets no cookies, has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. It only counts page views with a script that stores nothing on your device.

Website visits

This website counts page views with a cookie-free script that stores nothing on your device and sends only the page address, the referring website and the country (derived from your network address, which is not kept). It is switched off if your browser sends Do Not Track.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host receives your network address when your browser asks for a page, and may keep it in its own server logs under its own policy. We do not receive those logs.

Video

Videos on this site are files that we host ourselves. Nothing is loaded from a video service. A video starts loading only when you press play. Some entries also link to a video on YouTube; that is an ordinary link, and YouTube applies its own policy if you follow it.

Links to other websites

The links to Google Play, to the website of each app, to kalmarium.com and to YouTube lead to other sites. Links to Google Play carry a campaign tag (utm_source=hub) so that we can see which page an install came from. The tag does not identify you. Each app has its own privacy policy on its own website.

Your rights

Because we keep no personal data about visitors to this site, there is usually nothing to access, correct or delete. If you think we hold data about you, write to us and we will answer. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: kalmariumservices@gmail.com.
