How we build

  1. 01No account to start

    Every app can be played or tried without signing up. Knowdrop only creates a simple online account if you pick a nickname for friends and leaderboards.

  2. 02No forced subscription

    Eight of the nine apps have no subscription. Knowdrop offers an optional Plus subscription, and it is never needed to play.

  3. 03Small ads only

    Where an app shows ads, they are small banners that stay out of the way, and a one-time purchase removes them. Bilingua and Crack A Fold have none.

  4. 04Prices stated up front

    What is free, what costs money and what an ad-free purchase does is written on this site and on each Google Play listing.
