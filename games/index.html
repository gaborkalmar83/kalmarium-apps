Offline Android games: trivia, mahjong and a tank shooter

Three games for different moods: a daily trivia quiz to play alone or with friends, a calm mahjong solitaire with big tiles, and an arcade tank shooter with a full campaign. All three can be played without an internet connection and without an account. Looking for a game that teaches something? Lingua Pairs, a word-matching memory game, lives under Learning.

01

Knowdrop - daily trivia quiz

A fresh Daily Drop of 10 fact-checked questions every day, plus picture rounds and friend battles

Knowdrop gives you a new trivia round every day. The Daily Drop is ten questions, from easy to hard, and everyone gets the same ten, so you can compare scores with friends. Between drops there are quick rounds in 13 categories, themed packs, Picture Reveal, Speed Run and Sudden Death. Questions are written with AI help and then checked by a separate fact-check, and every answer comes with a fun fact and a source link. Play alone, pass one phone around, or host an online game that friends join with a code, a QR code or a PIN. It works offline, and there are no lives, no energy and no waiting timers.

What you get

  • Daily Drop: 10 questions, easy to hard, the same for everyone
  • 13 categories, 12,000+ questions, flag and picture quizzes
  • Plays offline; battle friends with a room code or QR code

Who it is for

Quiz fans who like a short daily habit, and friends or families who want a quick trivia game together.

Price

Free, with ads; optional purchases turn ads off

02

Mahjong365 - daily mahjong solitaire

Calm mahjong solitaire with a new level for every day of the year, large tiles and no pop-up ads

Mahjong365 is classic mahjong solitaire played the quiet way. Match two identical free tiles, clear the board and move on, with no timer that makes you fail. There is a level for every day of the year, spread over twelve worlds drawn in a soft ink and paper style, from a snow garden to a lantern festival. A Daily Challenge gives every player the same board, with streaks and monthly badges, and Keen Eye mode asks you to find the true pair among near-identical tiles. Tiles are large and symbols bold, so it suits long evenings and tired eyes. It plays offline, with one small banner and one optional lifetime Premium purchase.

What you get

  • 365 levels in 12 worlds, plus a new Daily Challenge
  • Large, clear tiles; unlimited undo and free shuffle
  • Plays offline; one small banner, one optional Premium

Who it is for

Relaxed puzzle players, including anyone who wants big tiles and a calm screen.

Price

Free, one small banner, no pop-up ads; optional lifetime Premium

Get it on Google PlayMore about Mahjong365

03

Tankageddon - offline tank shooter

One tank against the alien invasion: 99 levels, 99 boss fights, all playable offline

In 2091 the aliens came, and one tank is left. Tankageddon is a top-down tank shooter: drive with one thumb, aim and fire with the other, and fight your way from a fallen city to the heart of the Hive. Every map ends with a different boss, 99 in all, and the missions in between ask you to storm alien bases, escort supply trucks and free captured humans. Upgrade cannon, armour, shield and engine, grab alien tech that melts or swarms your enemies, and play by day or in pitch-black night. The whole campaign works offline and progress stays on your device. Art and audio were made with the help of AI tools.

What you get

  • 10 worlds, day and night missions, 99 different alien bosses
  • Upgrade your tank and grab short-lived alien tech
  • Twin-stick controls, 3 layouts, no account, no internet needed

Who it is for

Arcade shooter fans who want a full campaign on a phone, foldable or tablet, with no connection needed.

Price

Free, small banner; one-time Premium removes ads

Tankageddon
Tankageddon for arcade action with boss fights.

Which of these games work offline?

All three. Knowdrop and Mahjong365 play offline, and the whole Tankageddon campaign plays without internet. Knowdrop's friends, leaderboards and live parties need a connection.

Are they free?

Yes, all three are free to start. Knowdrop and Tankageddon show a small banner ad and Mahjong365 shows one small banner with no pop-up ads. A purchase removes the ads in each: Knowdrop offers Remove ads forever, Mahjong365 a lifetime Premium and Tankageddon a one-time Premium.

Do I need an account?

No. Mahjong365 and Tankageddon need no account, and progress stays on your device. Knowdrop can be played without one; picking a nickname and avatar creates a simple online account for friends and leaderboards, with no email address or password.

Which one should I start with?

Start with Knowdrop for a daily habit or a quiz night with friends, Mahjong365 for a calm puzzle with large tiles, and Tankageddon for arcade action with boss fights.
