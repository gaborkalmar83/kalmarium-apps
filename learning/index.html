Learning apps for Android: languages and Microsoft exams

Three apps for learning by doing. Lingua Pairs builds vocabulary with a memory game, Bilingua lets you read your own books with a translation under every sentence, and Prep 4 Cert prepares you for Microsoft certification exams with original practice questions. None of them needs an account or a subscription.

04

Lingua Pairs - language memory game

The memory game where you match a word with its translation and get a photo card with sound

Lingua Pairs turns the classic memory card game into vocabulary practice. Instead of two identical cards you match a word in your language with its translation. When you find a pair, the two cards merge into one picture card with a real photo, both words and the spoken pronunciation, so you see, read and hear the word together. Choose any pair of six languages, pick levels from A1 to C1 and eighteen categories, or import your own Anki decks. Rounds are short enough for a coffee break, and you can play alone or at an online table with two to four friends. Word lists were created with AI help and checked automatically.

What you get

  • German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian or English, any pair
  • 2,200+ words per language, six levels from A1 to C1
  • Import your own Anki decks or two-column word lists

Who it is for

Language learners who like short, playful practice, and visual learners who remember a word better with a photo.

Price

Free, small banner; one-time purchase removes ads

05

Bilingua - bilingual book reader

Read your own EPUB and PDF books with every sentence translated underneath, even offline

Bilingua is a book reader for people who learn a language by reading. Open any EPUB or PDF of your own and every sentence appears with its translation directly underneath, on its own line, on the same line or hidden until you tap. Translation runs on your phone with offline packs for 59 languages, so it works without a connection. Long-press a word to see its meaning and the sentence it came from, save it, and review it in the built-in flashcards or send it to AnkiDroid. There are no ads, no account and no tracking. Try everything free for 14 days, then unlock once with a single payment. There is no subscription.

What you get

  • Translation under each sentence; offline packs for 59 languages
  • Long-press a word to look it up, save it, send it to Anki
  • No ads, no account; 14-day free trial, then pay once

Who it is for

Language learners who read books and want translations and flashcards without leaving the page.

Price

No ads; 14-day free trial, then a one-time purchase

06

Prep 4 Cert - Microsoft exam practice

Original practice questions for 47 Microsoft certification exams, with a free 15-question preview each

Prep 4 Cert is a study app for Microsoft certification exams, from AZ-900 and AZ-104 to SC-900 and PL-300, 47 exams in all. Every question is written fresh from the exam's official skills outline, so you learn the subject instead of memorising answers. It is not an exam dump. Each answer has a short and a long explanation and a Study next pointer to the official documentation. Build tests of 10 to 100 questions, choose from four answer modes, repeat your mistakes and follow your progress by topic. Every exam has a free 15-question preview, and a full bank is a one-time purchase with no subscription. An independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

What you get

  • Answers that teach, with a Study next link to Microsoft Learn
  • Tests of 10 to 100 questions; repeat the ones you missed
  • Free job interview practice for four roles

Who it is for

IT professionals and students preparing for a Microsoft exam. Questions are drafted with AI assistance, checked against Microsoft's documentation and reviewed by the author, a Microsoft Certified Trainer.

Price

Free preview of 15 questions per exam; one-time purchase per bank; small banner in the free version

Original practice questions, not an exam dump. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

Which one should I pick?

Lingua Pairs
Lingua Pairs to build vocabulary in short, playful rounds.
Bilingua
Bilingua to learn a language by reading your own books.
Prep 4 Cert
Prep 4 Cert if you are preparing for a Microsoft exam. Independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

Which app is best for learning vocabulary?

Lingua Pairs. You match a word with its translation, and each pair becomes a photo card with the spoken word. It covers German, Spanish, French, Dutch, Hungarian and English. Bilingua suits you better if you want to learn from real books.

Can I read my own books in two languages?

Yes, with Bilingua. It opens your own EPUB and PDF files and shows every sentence with its translation underneath. Offline translation packs cover 59 languages. Books with DRM cannot be opened, and a scanned PDF without a text layer cannot be translated.

Is Prep 4 Cert an exam dump?

No. Every question is written fresh from the official skills outline of the exam, and nothing is copied from a real exam. It is an independent study aid, not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft.

Do these apps need an account or a subscription?

No account for any of them, and no subscription. Lingua Pairs and Prep 4 Cert are free with a small banner that a one-time purchase removes. Bilingua has no ads, a 14-day free trial and then one payment.
