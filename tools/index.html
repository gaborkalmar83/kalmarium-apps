Android tools and fun: WiFi analyzer, family intercom and a fold prank

Two practical apps and one joke. Ultimate Network Troubleshooter finds out what is wrong with your WiFi, Lazy Intercom turns your family's phones into a voice intercom, and Crack A Fold makes a foldable phone look broken for a moment. None of them needs an account.

07

Ultimate Network Troubleshooter - WiFi analyzer and network tools

WiFi analyzer, speed test, port scanner and network map in one Android app, with no account

Ultimate Network Troubleshooter works out what is wrong with your WiFi, which channel is best, how fast your connection really is and what is on your network. It starts with a plain-language view, with an Advanced switch for dBm and channel widths. You get a channel analyzer for 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz, a 4G and 5G antenna map, a speed test with ping and jitter, and a walk mode that maps signal strength around your home. The scanner lists devices and open ports, and the network map saves as a PDF or a draw.io file. There is no account, and what the app measures stays on your phone. Only scan networks you own or may test.

What you get

  • WiFi channel analyzer and one network health score
  • Speed test, walk-around heat map, device and port scanner
  • Network map exports to PDF or draw.io; iPerf3, DNS and Whois

Who it is for

Home users who want to know why the WiFi is slow, and IT or network people who want quick checks in their pocket.

Price

Free, one small banner; one-time purchase removes it

Lazy Intercom - family intercom app

Hold a button, speak, and the family hears it, even with headphones in and the screen off

Lazy Intercom turns the Android phones in your home into a voice intercom. Install it on each family phone, join with a QR code, then hold the button and speak. The other phones wake up, play a short sound and then your actual voice, even with the screen off and earbuds in,. If nobody taps Got it within 45 seconds, the message replays once, louder. Messages are encrypted end to end, travel directly between phones over your home WiFi and need no account or server of ours. It can also play on Google Cast, DLNA and Home Assistant speakers. It is Android only, and messages are recorded clips, not a live call.

What you get

  • Plays through earbuds and wakes a locked phone with a Got it card
  • No accounts; phones find each other on your home WiFi
  • Also plays on Google Cast, DLNA and Home Assistant speakers

Who it is for

Households on one WiFi, especially with teens in headphones, several floors or a home office. It is a new app, so feedback from real households is welcome.

Price

Free, one small banner; Remove ads is EUR 1.99 once

Crack A Fold - foldable phone prank

A prank for foldable phones: they fold it, the screen seems to crack. Nothing is damaged.

Crack A Fold is a prank for foldable Android phones. Arm it, hand your phone to a friend, and the moment they fold it they hear a sharp crack, feel a short jolt and see a broken crease across the big screen. The hinge position is measured on your own device, so the crack follows your real fold line, and you can pick a hairline crack, a black line, spreading ink or a dead half. You choose when it triggers, with a grace period before it arms. Nothing is damaged and no settings change: repair it with a tap on the four corners or an auto-repair timer. It is a paid app with no ads and no data collection.

What you get

  • The crack lands on your phone's own crease, with sound and a jolt
  • You choose when it breaks; repair it with one tap
  • No ads, no accounts, no data, no internet connection

Who it is for

Owners of foldable phones with a sense of humour and a friend who is easy to fool.

Price

Paid, about EUR 1.99 (varies by country); no ads, no data

Lazy Intercom to call the family without shouting.

Crack A Fold if you own a foldable phone and want a harmless prank.

Is it legal to scan a network with Ultimate Network Troubleshooter?

Only scan networks you own or have permission to test. The device and port scanners send packets to the hosts you choose. What the app measures stays on your phone, and there is no account.

Does Lazy Intercom need internet?

Not for messages. On your home WiFi the phones talk directly to each other, with no server of ours in between. The free version shows one small banner that needs a connection to load. It is Android only.

Does Crack A Fold damage my phone?

No. It only draws a fake crack on top of the screen and plays a sound and a short vibration. Nothing is damaged and no settings change. You repair it with a tap on the four corners or an auto-repair timer.

What do these apps cost?

Ultimate Network Troubleshooter and Lazy Intercom are free with one small banner, and a one-time purchase removes it (EUR 1.99 for Lazy Intercom). Crack A Fold is a paid app, about EUR 1.99 and it varies by country, with no ads.
